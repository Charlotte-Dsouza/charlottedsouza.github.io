Norway, brand health

Rebuilding Top-of-Mind in Oslo

Situation
foodora's Top-of-Mind awareness and customer penetration had dropped in Oslo, the market's most valuable city and the one setting the national number.
What I did
Pivoted the offline brand strategy to concentrate weight on Oslo specifically rather than spreading nationally, and rebalanced the channel mix behind it.
Result
National Top-of-Mind recovered 7 percentage points to a two-year high, with Oslo up 6 points, while category rivals moved the other way.
Reading
City-level share is a different problem from national share, and averaging the two hides it.
Turkey, campaign

Yemeksepeti's 25th anniversary

Situation
Q2 is structurally soft for recall in the Turkish food category. Two competitors were launching super-apps with heavy, long-format campaigns in the same window, and a major football tournament was competing for the same attention.
What I did
Built the anniversary platform and took it nationwide from 20 April: TV as the backbone with a high prime-time weighting, streets turned pink through a mass-reach OOH campaign across Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir and summer destinations, an anniversary sound extended into audio, creator-led social, and lower-third integrations inside live football broadcasts.
Result
Top-of-Mind up 3 points, the only food brand in the market to rebuild recall that wave. Category share-of-voice leadership held throughout the quarter despite both competitor launches.
What I would change
Creator-led and UGC formats massively outperformed standard reach buys on view-through and click-through, so the split should have leaned that way from day one. Brand lift study results were muddied by control-group contamination, an inherent problem when a campaign is genuinely everywhere at once.
EU, go to market

The foodora rebrand rollout

Situation
A full brand relaunch across eight European markets, live May 2023, with different local starting points, media landscapes and levels of existing brand equity.
What I did
Set the media objectives and campaign thresholds each market had to hit, ran the top-down budget allocation and the optimisation exercise that followed, briefed the agencies, and held the process from campaign brief through media plan approval to results reporting.
Result
Simultaneous relaunch across eight markets on schedule, with seven of eight clearing the global campaign health framework on GRP delivery and net reach.
What I would change
Awareness-first buying pushed response rates below our usual benchmark, which was the trade we accepted. The clearer lesson was on spot length and break timing: shorter spots in high-index months, and shorter gaps between flights, both protect cost KPIs without costing reach.
Hungary, partnerships

Bringing Auchan into quick commerce

Situation
A newly launched grocery partner needed to build awareness fast in a category where the main competitor had a long head start, and the internal fear was that pushing one partner would simply cannibalise the others.
What I did
Built a full-funnel activation around the partner, combining in-app and CRM mechanics with an ATL layer: national TV, radio, cinema across 18 theatres, nearly 240 static and digital OOH placements across eight cities, elevator advertising, influencer collaborations and online branding.
Result
Partner order growth of 142 percent month on month, overtaking the main competitor five months after launch, with quick-commerce GMV up 18 percent month on month and no cannibalisation of other grocery partners.
Reading
A partner campaign done properly lifts the category, not just the partner. That result is what turned the format into a repeatable one.
Global, governance

Making measurement standard, not heroic

Situation
Measurement quality varied by region, so campaign post-mortems were not comparable and learnings did not travel.
What I did
Sat in the core group of a cross-regional media agency review covering Europe, Turkey and APAC and led the Europe and Turkey scope, set global TV and OOH thresholds with a quarterly and bi-annual audit cycle, and worked with the data analytics team on the modelling that sat underneath it.
Result
A shared measurement and planning language across regions. New markets now start from the framework instead of from scratch.

Playbooks & analysis

The things that outlast the campaign.

Campaigns end. Frameworks keep working. These are the pieces I built or co-built at Delivery Hero so that the next market, and the next planner, did not have to start from a blank page.

Framework

Media Principles, Pandora

The planning rulebook for brand media across the Pandora brands. Sets how equity and promotional messaging should be balanced so that short-term response does not quietly erode long-term salience, defines launch versus sustain phasing with reach and frequency thresholds per channel, holds market-level reach curves and minimum delivery levels, and splits investment between food and quick commerce according to how ready each market actually is. Written so a local planner can build a compliant plan without needing a call.

Q1 2026
Agency review

Cross-regional media agency RFP

A media agency review spanning Europe, Turkey and APAC. I sat in the core project group across the full scope and led the Europe and Turkey side, writing the market pitch briefs, setting the strategic questions each agency had to answer, and working through both pitch rounds. APAC ran in parallel under a counterpart. Scope went from TV and OOH planning, buying and execution through online branding to a single reporting and impact measurement framework across every market. The aim was central buying leverage without flattening local flexibility.

2025
Playbook

How to nail affordability on TV

Promotional and value messaging is the hardest thing to do well on TV: too much price and the brand disappears, too little and nobody acts. Sorted the approaches into three creative routes, from pure promo through to dedicated creative, with the effort and payoff of each set out honestly.

Playbook

Nailing affordability on brand media 2.0

The follow-up, widened from TV to the whole brand media mix. Central finding: the size of the offer is often not what drives performance. How it is said and shown matters as much, minimal text keeps attention on brand cues, and long-term incentive structures beat one-off discounts on brand connection.

Playbook

30 inspirational activations

An idea bank of offline and experiential activations for local marketing teams, indexed by budget and by production complexity, so a market with fifteen thousand euros and a market with a real budget can both find something buildable. Each entry carries its honest pros and cons.

September 2022
Analysis, with the data analytics team

TV on and off analysis

What actually happens to a market when TV goes dark. Daily sessions and acquisitions across two and a half years, decomposed into trend, seasonality and noise, then compared across matched before, during and after windows. Used to argue for flight patterns rather than continuous spend.

Europe, 2022
Analysis, with the data analytics team

Offline spend and quick-commerce Top-of-Mind

Correlation study across a full year of monthly data, testing offline brand spend against quick-commerce sessions, acquisitions and trials. Written up with its own limitations stated up front, because a correlation study that hides its shortcomings is worse than no study.

Europe, 2023
Audit & framework

TV audit and brand media measurement

The global audit layer: campaign delivery against GRP and reach thresholds per market, ad-fatigue curves plotting response rate against campaign duration, and media mix model outputs read alongside them. This is where the planning thresholds in the rebrand case study came from.
