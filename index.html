How I work

Plan, run, prove.

Most brand roles are strong on one of these. The interesting work happens when the same person carries all three, because the measurement changes the plan.

Plan

Local insight into the annual plan

Competitive landscape, category dynamics and cultural nuance per market — then an annual brand plan and budget split that reflects them, rather than a global template pushed down.

Run

360° execution across ATL and BTL

TV, OOH, radio, cinema, OTT/VOD, paid social, influencer and retail media. Briefing creative, steering media and creative agencies, and keeping ten markets on one timeline.

Prove

Measurement that changes decisions

MMM, incrementality and correlation analysis, brand tracking, share of voice and social listening — read together, and turned into the next quarter's plan.

Norway · Brand health

Rebuilding Top-of-Mind in Oslo

Situation
foodora's Top-of-Mind awareness and customer penetration had dropped in Oslo — the market's most valuable city and the one setting the national number.
What I did
Pivoted the offline brand strategy to concentrate weight on Oslo specifically rather than spreading nationally, and rebalanced the channel mix behind it.
Result
National Top-of-Mind recovered 7 percentage points to a two-year high, with Oslo up 6 points — while category rivals moved the other way.
Reading
City-level share is a different problem from national share, and averaging the two hides it.
Turkey · Campaign

Yemeksepeti's 25th anniversary

Situation
Q2 is structurally soft for recall in the Turkish food category — the brand had historically given ground in that wave.
What I did
Built the 25th-anniversary platform and took it nationwide across every ATL channel, turning the country red for the duration.
Result
Top-of-Mind up 3 points — the only food brand in the market to rebuild recall that wave, against a category-wide slide.
Next
Unaided awareness is already the category's highest; the work now is converting that into first mention.

Partnerships · 360°

Co-marketing with restaurant & grocery brands

Situation
Partner brands wanted reach; we wanted acquisition. Joint campaigns tend to stall because neither side will give up creative control.
What I did
Built a repeatable 360° co-marketing format — shared creative territory, split channel plan, agreed KPIs on both sides — and scaled it beyond a single activation.
Result
20% month-on-month increase in customer acquisition from the scaled activation.

Global · Governance

Making measurement standard, not heroic

Situation
Measurement quality varied by region, so campaign post-mortems weren't comparable and learnings didn't travel.
What I did
Led multi-regional agency pitches across APAC, EU and Turkey; implemented MMM, incrementality and correlation analysis as standard; authored playbooks on cross-channel media principles, affordability messaging, influencer activation and retail media.
Result
A shared measurement and planning language across regions — new markets start from the playbook instead of from scratch.
