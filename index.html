Norway, brand health

Rebuilding Top-of-Mind in Oslo

Situation
foodora's Top-of-Mind awareness and customer penetration dropped in Oslo, the market's most valuable city and the one that drives national performance.
What I did
Pivoted the ATL strategy to concentrate weight directly on Oslo rather than diluting impact nationally, rebalancing the channel mix behind it.
Result
National Top-of-Mind recovered 7 percentage points to a two-year high (with Oslo up 6 points), while category rivals declined.
Key Takeaway
City-level share is a distinct problem from national share, and averaging the two hides it.
Turkey, campaign

Yemeksepeti's 25th anniversary

Situation
Q2 is structurally soft for recall in the Turkish food delivery category. Two competitors launched super-apps with heavy campaigns simultaneously, alongside peak football tournament ad clutter.
What I did
Built and executed the anniversary strategy and took it nationwide from 20 April: TV as the backbone with a high prime-time weighting, streets turned pink through a mass-reach OOH campaign across Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir and summer destinations, an anniversary jingle that extended into audio, creator-led social, and lower-third integrations inside live football broadcasts.
Result
Top-of-Mind up by 3 points, the only food brand in the market to rebuild recall during that wave, while holding SOV leadership throughout.
What I would change
Creator-led and UGC formats heavily outperformed standard reach buys on view-through and click-through rates, we should have allocated more spend there upfront. Additionally, broad campaign saturation created control-group contamination in brand lift studies.
EU, go to market

The foodora rebrand rollout

Situation
A simultaneous brand relaunch across eight European markets, live May 2023, with varying brand equity, starting points, and media landscapes.
What I did
Defined market-level media objectives and thresholds, managed top-down budget allocations, briefed creative/media agencies, and steered execution from brief to post-campaign reporting.
Result
Launched on schedule across all eight markets, with seven out of eight hitting global health benchmarks for GRP delivery and net reach.
What I would observed
Prioritizing awareness pushed response rates below baseline—an acceptable trade-off given the campaign objective. However, shorter spot lengths in high-index months and tighter flighting gaps would have better protected cost KPIs without sacrificing reach.
Hungary, partnerships

Driving Q-Commerce Traction with a Flagship Retail Partner

Situation
The need to scale Quick Commerce vertical by co-marketing with a high-value national retail partner. We had to establish rapid market awareness against an established competitor while proving that driving volume for a major partner would expand the overall category rather than cannibalize our broader merchant base.
What I did
Built a full-funnel partner activation connecting in-app mechanics with high-impact ATL (national TV, radio, cinema across 18 theaters, 240 OOH placements, elevator ads, and digital branding).
Result
Partner orders grew 142% MoM, surpassing the main competitor within five months, with quick-commerce GMV up 18 percent month on month and no cannibalisation of other grocery partners.
Key Takeaway
A partner campaign done properly lifts the category, not just the partner. That result is what turned the format into a repeatable one.
Global, governance

Making measurement standard, not heroic

Situation
Inconsistent regional measurement models meant campaign post-mortems were not comparable across hubs and learnings failed to scale.
What I did
Co-led the agency review scope for Europe and Turkey in a cross-regional media review (EU, TR, APAC), established global TV/OB thresholds with structured audit cycles, and aligned data analytics teams on underlying attribution models.
Result
Established a unified planning and measurement framework across regions, enabling new markets to launch from a proven standard instead of building from scratch.

Supporting talabat & HungerStation

Playbooks & analysis

The things that outlast the campaign.

Campaigns end. Frameworks keep working. These are the frameworks and playbooks I built (or co-built) at Delivery Hero so local planners and new markets never have to start from a blank page.

Framework

Media Principles, Pandora

The planning rulebook for brand media across the Pandora brands. Balances brand equity with promotional messaging to protect long-term salience, defines launch vs. sustain reach and frequency thresholds per channel, and maps investment between food and quick-commerce based on market maturity, allowing local planners to build compliant plans independently.

2026
Agency review

Cross-regional media agency RFP

Co-led a multi-regional media agency review spanning Europe, Turkey, and APAC. Led the EU and Turkey scope: drafting pitch briefs, setting evaluation criteria, and steering both pitch rounds. Standardized TV, OOH, digital, and reporting frameworks across all markets to maximize central buying leverage while preserving local agility.

2025
Playbook

How to nail affordability on TV

Promotional and value messaging is the hardest thing to do well on TV: too much price and the brand disappears, hide the discount and conversion drops. Structured three distinct creative execution routes, from pure promo to brand-led—with transparent breakdowns of production effort versus commercial payoff.

2022
Playbook

Nailing affordability on brand media 2.0

Expanded the TV playbook across the entire brand media mix. Key takeaway: the size of the offer is often not what drives performance. How it is said and shown matters as much, Minimal on-screen text protects brand recall, and long-term incentive models consistently beat one-off promotional spikes.

2024
Playbook

30 inspirational activations

A tactical activation bank for local marketing hubs, indexed by budget tier and execution complexity. Designed so markets with €15k can execute as effectively as those with major budgets, complete with honest pros, cons, and operational trade-offs for every entry.

2022
Analysis, with the data analytics team

TV on and off analysis

Evaluated what happens to a market when TV goes dark across 2.5 years of daily session and acquisition data. Decomposed trend, seasonality and noise across pre, mid, and post-campaign windows, providing the empirical case for flighted campaign bursts over continuous spend.

2022
Analysis, with the data analytics team

Offline spend and quick-commerce Top-of-Mind

A 12-month correlation analysis evaluating offline brand spend against quick-commerce sessions, new customer acquisitions, and trials. Documented transparently with methodology constraints upfront, proving where offline media directly drives digital conversions.

2023
Audit & framework

TV audit and brand media measurement

The global governance layer tracking market delivery against GRP and reach targets, ad-fatigue response curves, and MMM outputs. Established the benchmark thresholds that powered the multi-market foodora rebrand.
